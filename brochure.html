Project Summary & Analysis

BuildCon ERP is a state-of-the-art enterprise construction management platform. It enables real-time collaboration between corporate managers, site engineers, finance departments, subcontractors, and HR personnel. The platform scopes all operations per organization, offering a highly responsive multi-tenant SaaS environment.

Architecture Layer Technology Stack Core Functionality
Frontend Next.js 14, TS, Tailwind CSS Dynamic multi-dashboard portal, real-time OTP lookup, charts, dark mode UI.
Backend Spring Boot 3, Java 17, Security Enterprise REST APIs, JWT token authorization, role-based access control.
AI Engine FastAPI Python, LangChain Automated post-project performance audits, site delay diagnosis, cash flow forecasting.
Database MySQL 8.x Relational structure tracking user credentials, ledger balances, materials, and workforce.

Key Pipelines

  • OTP Gate: Deterministic time-based 4-digit code generated dynamically for safe tenant selection.
  • Progress Claims Flow: Subcontractor Requisition ➔ Quantity Surveyor Verification ➔ Finance Release (Paid / Hold).
  • Delay Alert Pipeline: Site delays uploaded by engineers trigger real-time critical banners on the Chairman portal.

Role Matrices by Tier (Growth & Premium)

Growth Tier (Field Operations & Site Execution)
Project Manager (PM)
Manages project milestones, schedules tasks via Gantt charts, drafts progress claims, reports site delays, and creates material requisitions.
Construction Manager (CM)
Audits site execution logs, oversees concrete compression (cube) testing, tracks heavy equipment (cranes, machinery), and records daily diary logs.
Senior Site Engineer (SSE)
Performs site measurements, handles daily engineer log entries, registers safety issues, and raises site delay alerts to trigger executive warnings.
Subcontractor / Labour
Subcontractors file monthly work progress claims and upload invoices. Workers / Labour provide on-site manpower for direct execution.
Premium Tier (Financial Auditing & Support)

Includes all Growth Tier features, plus professional support portals:

Finance & Accounts Manager
Controls the ledger, adds receivables, manages payables, generates cash flow forecasts, releases approved subcontractor payouts, and monitors tax compliance.
Quantity Surveyor (QS)
Maintains Bill of Quantities (BOQ), performs material measurements, reviews progress claims to approve or reject them, and reports cost variances.
HR Manager
Manages employee profiles, schedules recruitments/interviews, processes payroll/deductions, performs appraisals, and monitors labor law compliance.
Procurement Manager
Handles purchase requisitions, triggers RFQs to verified vendors, creates Purchase Orders, tracks deliveries, and controls raw materials inventory.

Role Matrices by Tier (Enterprise)

Enterprise Tier (Executive Portals & Marketing)

Unlocks full platform features, including executive-level overview and growth tools:

Chairman
Monitors organization KPIs (budget value, active staff, cash flow), receives critical safety and delay alerts, reviews board documents, and queries the AI Strategy Assistant.
Managing Director (MD)
Oversees operations, reviews department performance reports, runs approval queues, and performs high-level audits.
Directors (Project / Business / Finance)
Review broad portfolio progress, strategic partner relationships, business sales pipelines, and financial budgets.
Marketing & Sales Team (Manager / TL / Exec)
Marketing Managers track ROI; Digital Marketing leads handle campaigns and social channels; Sales Executives manage client proposals and CRM leads.
Workforce Manager
Audits daily manpower headcount, verifies subcontractor Aadhaar profiles, and coordinates crew payouts with accounting.
Super Admin
Platform-level administrator managing database instances, creating organization tenants, mapping subscriptions, and configuring dynamic AI modules.
