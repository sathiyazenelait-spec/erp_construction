Subscription Tier Controls

BuildCon ERP maps system visibility dynamically. Upon OTP verification, the active subscription tier context is saved in client localStorage. On role login, routes and selection dropdowns are dynamically parsed. If a user bypasses client-side Auth guards with a direct URL modification, the backend JWT filter intercepts and blocks requests with an HTTP 403 Forbidden response.

Project Manager (PM)

Growth Tier

Controls project schedules and timelines via dynamic Gantt engines, coordinates material requisitions, and initiates Progress Claims based on milestones.

What They View:
  • Gantt charts with scheduled milestones & progress percentages.
  • Stock inventory levels (sand, steel, cement) from procurement.
  • Real-time progress claims statuses (Pending, Approved, Paid, Hold).
What They Do/Post:
  • Claim Builder: Logs completion metrics, starts claim workflows.
  • Requisition Form: Posts material requests to Procurement.
  • Site Delay Logs: Triggers delay warnings on Chairman portal.
Set Gantt Milestones
Submit Requisition Request
Log Site Delay (If occurs)
Submit Progress Claim

What They Do/Post:
  • Concrete Test Logs: Records cube compression values.
  • Daily Site Diary: Submits weather, worker log, & activity forms.
  • Machinery Schedule: Updates equipment runtimes and fuel logs.

Labour & Workers

Growth Tier

Site laborers do not access dashboards directly. Instead, their identities are managed through local gates and their profiles are maintained by the Workforce Manager.

  • Skill Adjustments: Workforce Manager updates trade classifications (Masons, Carpenters, Helpers).

Log Entry & Exit Times

Finance & Accounts Manager

Premium Tier

Controls corporate accounts, registers client receivables, cash flow forecasting charts, and handles the release of payment vouchers for approved claims.

What They View:
  • General ledger transaction history (Receivables & Payables).
  • Cash flow trends comparing inflows against outflows.
  • Progress claim queue showing status APPROVED from QS.
What They Do/Post:
  • Add Receivable Form: Registers new client debtor invoice totals.
  • Payout Release: Clicks release to disburse progress claims (POSTs to `/api/progress-claims/{id}/pay`), changing status to PAID.
  • Payment Hold: Places claims on hold, setting status to HOLD.
Audit General Ledger
Register Accounts Receivable
Disburse Payment Voucher
Mark Claim Status "PAID"

Quantity Surveyor (QS)

Premium Tier

Manages estimated quantities, audits Bill of Quantities (BOQ), and acts as the gatekeeper for subcontractor claim validations.

What They View:
  • Estimated BOQ vs actual quantity measurements on-site.
  • Pending subcontractor progress claims queue.
  • Actual deliveries & local site material intake records.
What They Do/Post:
  • Claim Verification: Approves claims (PUTs `/api/progress-claims/{id}/approve`) to push them to the Finance manager's release queue.
  • Claim Rejection: Rejects claims, logging reasons for subcontractors.
  • BOQ Form Updates: Edits material quantity and cost estimates.
Review Subcontractor Claim
Compare Claim with BOQ Limits
Approve Progress Claim
Push Claim to Finance Queue

AI Strategy & Compliance Integration

AI Pipeline

BuildCon ERP connects its Spring Boot Rest APIs directly with a dedicated Python FastAPI service running generative AI models. This integrates advanced operational automation including:

  • Executive Strategy: Scans raw ledger transactions to forecast cash flows.
  • Expense Audits: Detects vendor invoice variances and checks TDS compliance.
  • Workforce Planner: Optimizes worker scheduling based on Gantt milestones.
  • Project Quality: Analyzes concrete compressive test histories for anomalies.
